Real Estate Acquisition Manager

A location-bound search for an unusually broad real-estate profile combining acquisition, development and commercial understanding.

01THE CHALLENGE

Why the search was difficult.

The client needed an on-site “all-rounder” with experience across real-estate acquisition, project development and commercial work.

The market was narrow: many candidates were too specialised, while the Dresden/Leipzig on-site requirement reduced the available pool further.

02THE SEARCH

How we approached the market.

The strategy centred on targeted headhunting, direct outreach and market mapping rather than classic applications. LinkedIn Recruiter and AI-supported sourcing methods were used to identify passive candidates with the right mix of technical and commercial experience.

03THE OUTCOME

What the search produced.

50 targeted candidates were identified and 30 replied. More than 65% of responses were positive.

More than 70% of the positively responding candidates had not originally been actively open to a new position, showing the value of proactive search in a niche property market.
