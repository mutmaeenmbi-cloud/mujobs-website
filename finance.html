01

Industry-specific recruitment challenges

Similar finance titles can hide different levels of responsibility. Supporting a monthly close is not the same as owning it. Reporting within a group differs from building financial processes in a smaller business. A useful brief makes these distinctions visible before CVs arrive.

03

The evidence behind a relevant shortlist

We assess candidates against the responsibilities of the position. We agree the criteria with you for each assignment so that the shortlist reflects your specific requirements.

  • Reporting and closing responsibilities, including the level of review.
  • Relevant systems, data quality and process improvement experience.
  • Business model, stakeholder communication and practical ownership.
04

Map the market. Make the connection.

We look for comparable responsibilities, systems and company environments rather than assuming that every accounting background is interchangeable. Before the search starts, we clarify what must be present on day one and what can be learned. That distinction helps avoid an unnecessarily narrow brief while preserving the standards the role genuinely requires.

What the search can include

  • A focused briefing and role-specific market map.
  • Relevant profile research and personal candidate outreach.
  • Initial qualification, follow-ups and interview coordination.
  • A shortlist with clear reasoning and visible open questions.

Define the recruitment brief

Tell us about the position, location, team structure and the challenges you have faced in filling the vacancy. Together, we can distinguish what is essential from what is flexible and agree a search that fits the business.
