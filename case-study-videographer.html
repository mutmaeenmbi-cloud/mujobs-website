01 / The challenge

What the role demanded.

Videographer titles hide very different skill sets. The client needed someone with creative judgement as well as production capability: concept development, camera work, editing, storytelling and the ability to deliver content consistently for a commercial environment.

02 / Our approach

How we focused the search.

mujobs treated the portfolio as evidence. The market was mapped around relevant creative profiles and candidates were reviewed for style, production range, commercial work and the ability to own more than one stage of the content process.

03 / The outcome

What changed for the client.

59 relevant candidates were mapped into the search, creating a focused creative pipeline instead of a broad list of people who simply carried the Videographer title.
