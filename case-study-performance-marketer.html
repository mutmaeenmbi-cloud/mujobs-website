01 / The challenge

What the role demanded.

The client needed someone who could do more than operate ad platforms. The profile had to understand performance strategy, paid social/search, experimentation, tracking, analytics, creative testing and the commercial relationship between spend and growth.

Many candidates could show channel experience; fewer could show clear ownership of performance outcomes and explain how they improved them.

02 / Our approach

How we focused the search.

mujobs built the search around measurable evidence: budgets managed, channels owned, testing systems, attribution knowledge and examples of improving CAC, ROAS or conversion performance.

78 top profiles were shortlisted, allowing the client to compare candidates on results rather than marketing titles.

03 / The outcome

What changed for the client.

The active search was built in two weeks and the position was successfully closed in under three weeks.
