The hiring challenge

The startup needed more than a strong individual contributor. The target profile had to work across Vue.js and Node.js, understand SaaS product environments and be capable of leading engineers while remaining technically hands-on.

That combination narrowed the market significantly: many candidates were strong on one side of the stack, but fewer combined full-stack depth, SaaS context and team leadership.

02

The search approach

mujobs mapped engineers from relevant SaaS and product companies, prioritising evidence of modern JavaScript delivery, architecture decisions, mentoring and ownership of teams or technical workstreams.

The search focused on high-quality target companies and built a reusable talent pool rather than stopping at the first viable candidate.

For employers hiring a Senior Software Developer, MuJobs combines role-specific market mapping, active sourcing and direct candidate outreach. We clarify relevant experience, motivation and availability, then coordinate the next conversations around the hiring brief.

03

The outcome

The startup made the hire within one month. More than 100 qualified technology profiles remained in the pipeline, giving the company a strong starting point for future engineering growth.

04

Understanding the role

A senior full-stack role combines implementation with judgement. The person may need to work across the frontend and backend, make architecture decisions and support less experienced engineers. For a SaaS business, the assessment should also connect technical decisions to product needs, release reliability and the stage of the engineering team.

Questions that make the brief more precise

  • What did the person deliver across Vue.js and Node.js?
  • Which architecture and technical trade-offs did they own?
  • How did they combine hands-on work with mentoring or team leadership?
05

Planning a similar search

Start with the work the new person will own, the business context and the constraints that cannot change. Separate essential experience from preferences. That gives the market mapping a clear direction and makes it easier to explain why someone belongs on the shortlist.

A similar title is only a research starting point. In an initial conversation, we would clarify the project or team, the practical conditions and the evidence you need to make a decision. The scope of sourcing, candidate qualification and interview coordination can then be agreed around the actual requirement.
