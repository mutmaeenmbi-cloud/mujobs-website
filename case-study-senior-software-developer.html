01 / The challenge

What the role demanded.

The startup needed more than a strong individual contributor. The target profile had to work across Vue.js and Node.js, understand SaaS product environments and be capable of leading engineers while remaining technically hands-on.

That combination narrowed the market significantly: many candidates were strong on one side of the stack, but fewer combined full-stack depth, SaaS context and team leadership.

02 / Our approach

How we focused the search.

mujobs mapped engineers from relevant SaaS and product companies, prioritising evidence of modern JavaScript delivery, architecture decisions, mentoring and ownership of teams or technical workstreams.

The search focused on high-quality target companies and built a reusable talent pool rather than stopping at the first viable candidate.

03 / The outcome

What changed for the client.

The startup made the hire within one month. More than 100 qualified technology profiles remained in the pipeline, giving the company a strong starting point for future engineering growth.
