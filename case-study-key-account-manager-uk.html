01

The hiring challenge

The role required pharmaceutical or healthcare exposure, strategic account management, commercial judgement and the credibility to manage important partnerships. The relevant market was experienced, stable and largely passive.

02

The search approach

mujobs mapped the UK market around healthcare and pharmaceutical account leadership, then used direct, personalised outreach to create conversations with candidates who were not actively applying.

The focus was not application volume. It was access to commercially proven people with the right sector context.

For employers hiring a Senior Key Account Manager — Pharma, MuJobs combines role-specific market mapping, active sourcing and direct candidate outreach. We clarify relevant experience, motivation and availability, then coordinate the next conversations around the hiring brief.

03

The outcome

45 candidates were shortlisted, 33 replied and 27 showed positive interest — a 73% response rate. The search demonstrated how a passive market can become an active pipeline when the outreach is specific enough.

The recruitment project concluded with a completed hire.

04

Understanding the role

Healthcare account management combines commercial judgement with an understanding of the customer environment. Strategic account responsibility is more than maintaining contact: it can involve planning, relationship development and coordination across multiple stakeholders. Relevant experience must be interpreted within the specific market and type of partnership the role will manage.

Questions that make the brief more precise

  • Which customer groups and strategic accounts were managed?
  • What decisions and partnership outcomes did the person own?
  • How did they coordinate complex commercial relationships?
05

Planning a similar search

Start with the work the new person will own, the business context and the constraints that cannot change. Separate essential experience from preferences. That gives the market mapping a clear direction and makes it easier to explain why someone belongs on the shortlist.

A similar title is only a research starting point. In an initial conversation, we would clarify the project or team, the practical conditions and the evidence you need to make a decision. The scope of sourcing, candidate qualification and interview coordination can then be agreed around the actual requirement.
