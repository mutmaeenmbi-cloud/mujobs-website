01THE CHALLENGE

Why the search was difficult.

The role required healthcare or pharmaceutical exposure, account-management experience, commercial understanding, relationship skills and the ability to manage strategic partnerships.

Most relevant candidates were already established in stable organisations and were not actively applying.

02THE SEARCH

How we approached the market.

The search used deep sourcing, direct outreach and market mapping through LinkedIn Recruiter, AI-supported sourcing, Boolean/X-Ray search and targeted campaigns.

The focus was relevance and commercial fit rather than sourcing volume.

03THE OUTCOME

What the search produced.

45 candidates were deeply sourced and shortlisted. 33 replied, producing a 73% response rate, and 27 showed positive interest.

Around 58% of positively responding candidates said they were happy in their current company and were not actively applying — but were open to a strong opportunity when approached directly.
