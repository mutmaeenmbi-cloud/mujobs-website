01THE CHALLENGE

What made the role difficult.

The role required pharmaceutical or healthcare exposure, strategic account management, commercial judgement and the credibility to manage important partnerships. The relevant market was experienced, stable and largely passive.

02THE MUJOBS SEARCH

How we solved the search.

mujobs mapped the UK market around healthcare and pharmaceutical account leadership, then used direct, personalised outreach to create conversations with candidates who were not actively applying.

The focus was not application volume. It was access to commercially proven people with the right sector context.

03THE OUTCOME

What changed for the client.

45 candidates were shortlisted, 33 replied and 27 showed positive interest — a 73% response rate. The search demonstrated how a passive market can become an active pipeline when the outreach is specific enough.
