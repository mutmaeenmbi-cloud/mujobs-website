Targeted recruitment for software engineers, product specialists and customer success teams. We look at the product, technical environment and level of ownership behind each technology hire.

01

Where the search becomes difficult

A list of frameworks cannot tell you whether someone can work with your architecture, take ownership in a small team or lead other engineers. The difficult part is distinguishing familiarity with a technology from experience delivering reliable products in a comparable environment.

03

The evidence behind a relevant shortlist

Our starting questions connect the role to the work. The final assessment criteria are agreed for your particular brief, rather than applied as a generic checklist.

  • Product complexity, engineering team size and release responsibility.
  • Architecture decisions, testing habits and collaboration with product.
  • Technical depth, mentoring and the balance of leadership and hands-on work.
04

Map the market. Make the connection.

We map comparable SaaS businesses and product teams, then assess how the candidate’s work relates to your stage of growth. Technical screening should remain connected to the job: real trade-offs, examples of delivery and the decisions the new hire will need to make. A specialist interview can then go deeper without restarting the search from zero.

What the search can include

  • A focused briefing and role-specific market map.
  • Relevant profile research and personal candidate outreach.
  • Initial qualification, follow-ups and interview coordination.
  • A shortlist with clear reasoning and visible open questions.

Start with the brief

Share the responsibility you need to fill, the location, the team structure and what has made the role hard to close. Together, we can distinguish what is essential from what is flexible and agree a search that fits the business.
