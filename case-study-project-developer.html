01THE CHALLENGE

What made the role difficult.

The client needed someone who could think like both an architect and a developer: understand design and planning, but also feasibility, approvals, economics, stakeholder management and the commercial logic behind a development.

The project environment ranged from roughly CHF 20 million to CHF 200 million across Switzerland and Germany. The role had already been open for more than six months without the right combination of experience.

02THE MUJOBS SEARCH

How we solved the search.

mujobs widened the search beyond one title and mapped senior architects who had moved into development, developer-side project leaders and real-estate professionals with strong architectural foundations.

More than 100 high-relevance profiles were mapped and qualified around project scale, development responsibility, approvals, economics and the ability to operate across the Swiss/German market.

03THE OUTCOME

What changed for the client.

The position was successfully closed in three weeks. The client moved from a six-month vacancy to a hire and retained a deep specialist pipeline for future development roles.
