01THE CHALLENGE

What made the role difficult.

A smaller agency needs range. The strategist had to understand positioning, campaign thinking, client objectives and channel choices while being practical enough to turn strategy into briefs and execution with a lean team.

The challenge was finding people who were neither purely conceptual nor purely operational — and who would genuinely fit a smaller, hands-on agency environment.

02THE MUJOBS SEARCH

How we solved the search.

mujobs mapped strategists, senior marketers and agency-side profiles around client exposure, strategic ownership and hands-on execution. The search prioritised evidence of turning business problems into practical marketing direction.

80 relevant profiles were mapped into the market and qualified for agency fit.

03THE OUTCOME

What changed for the client.

The position was successfully closed in four weeks, giving the agency a strategist able to move between client conversations, planning and delivery.
