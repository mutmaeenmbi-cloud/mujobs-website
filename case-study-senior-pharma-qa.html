A niche senior-quality search across regulated pharmaceutical environments in Germany.

01THE CHALLENGE

Why the search was difficult.

The client needed a senior QA profile combining GMP/GDP expertise, pharmaceutical experience, leadership capability and regulatory/compliance understanding.

The combination of seniority and specialist QA expertise made the market small, while many qualified candidates were already employed and highly selective.

02THE SEARCH

How we approached the market.

The search combined LinkedIn Recruiter, AI-supported sourcing, Boolean/X-Ray search, direct outreach and focused market mapping across pharmaceutical, biotech and generic-pharma environments in Germany.

Candidate stages, responses and interview progress were tracked through structured pipeline reporting.

03THE OUTCOME

What the search produced.

60 pharmaceutical QA professionals were sourced, 40 replied, 25 responded positively and 10 entered interview stages.

More than 70% of positively responding candidates were not actively looking when approached, reinforcing the importance of targeted outreach in specialist pharmaceutical hiring.
