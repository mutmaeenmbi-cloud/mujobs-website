01 / The challenge

What the role demanded.

The client needed senior QA expertise with the right mix of GMP/GDP knowledge, pharmaceutical experience, regulatory judgement and leadership capability. Many qualified people were already employed and selective about their next move.

02 / Our approach

How we focused the search.

mujobs mapped pharmaceutical, biotech and generic-pharma environments and qualified profiles against the actual compliance and leadership evidence required by the role. Direct outreach and structured follow-up moved the search beyond the active applicant pool.

03 / The outcome

What changed for the client.

60 relevant QA professionals entered the market map, 40 replied, 25 were positive and 10 reached interview stage. The client gained both an active interview pipeline and a clearer picture of the senior QA market.
