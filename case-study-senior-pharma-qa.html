01

The hiring challenge

The client needed senior QA expertise with the right mix of GMP/GDP knowledge, pharmaceutical experience, regulatory judgement and leadership capability. Many qualified people were already employed and selective about their next move.

02

The search approach

mujobs mapped pharmaceutical, biotech and generic-pharma environments and qualified profiles against the actual compliance and leadership evidence required by the role. Direct outreach and structured follow-up moved the search beyond the active applicant pool.

03

The outcome

60 relevant QA professionals entered the market map, 40 replied, 25 were positive and 10 reached interview stage. The client gained both an active interview pipeline and a clearer picture of the senior QA market.

04

Understanding the role

Senior QA recruitment needs a precise understanding of the quality environment. GMP/GDP knowledge is a starting point; the scope of the role determines which systems, decisions and leadership responsibilities are relevant. The search should surface evidence for specialist interviewers to examine, without treating the appearance of regulatory keywords as proof of competence.

Questions that make the brief more precise

  • Which pharmaceutical settings and quality systems are directly relevant?
  • What regulatory and quality decisions were within the person’s remit?
  • How did they lead teams and resolve quality issues?
05

Planning a similar search

Start with the work the new person will own, the business context and the constraints that cannot change. Separate essential experience from preferences. That gives the market mapping a clear direction and makes it easier to explain why someone belongs on the shortlist.

A similar title is only a research starting point. In an initial conversation, we would clarify the project or team, the practical conditions and the evidence you need to make a decision. The scope of sourcing, candidate qualification and interview coordination can then be agreed around the actual requirement.
