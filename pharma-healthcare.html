Pharma recruitment for specialist responsibilities.

Selected search experience across pharmaceutical quality assurance and healthcare key account management. We distinguish technical expertise, regulatory context and commercial responsibility.

IN THIS PRACTICEThe hiring challengeRoles we coverWhat we assessHow we search
01

Where the search becomes difficult

Pharmaceutical recruitment can involve a small pool of professionals with highly specific experience. A senior quality assurance manager and a healthcare key account manager require very different evidence. The search must reflect the actual responsibilities rather than treating sector familiarity as sufficient.

03

The evidence behind a relevant shortlist

Our starting questions connect the role to the work. The final assessment criteria are agreed for your particular brief, rather than applied as a generic checklist.

  • For QA: relevant GMP/GDP context, quality systems and leadership responsibility.
  • For account roles: customer environment, partnership ownership and commercial judgement.
  • Evidence of the candidate’s actual decisions and scope, not sector labels alone.
04

Map the market. Make the connection.

We map relevant employers and adjacent environments, then qualify profiles against the specific brief. Our existing examples include a senior QA pipeline in Germany and a pharmaceutical account-management search in the UK. These are distinct search contexts; the market map and assessment questions should change accordingly.

What the search can include

  • A focused briefing and role-specific market map.
  • Relevant profile research and personal candidate outreach.
  • Initial qualification, follow-ups and interview coordination.
  • A shortlist with clear reasoning and visible open questions.

Start with the brief

Share the responsibility you need to fill, the location, the team structure and what has made the role hard to close. Together, we can distinguish what is essential from what is flexible and agree a search that fits the business.
