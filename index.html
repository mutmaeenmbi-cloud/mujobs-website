03 / THE mujobs METHOD

Understand first. Search second.

A disciplined seven-stage search framework, from understanding the business to strengthening what comes after the placement.

A SEVEN-STAGE FRAMEWORK

Understanding before action.
Quality throughout.

01

Understand Before You Search

Learn the business, its direction, challenges and the problem this hire must solve.

02

Define Success

Agree outcomes, essential capabilities, non-negotiables and qualities that matter.

03

Market Intelligence

Map target organisations, talent movements and where strong people are working.

04

Strategic Search

Search beyond active applicants and assess capability against long-term objectives.

05

Candidate Engagement

Create prepared, personalised conversations around motivations and mutual opportunity.

06

Evaluation + Partnership

Evaluate achievements, leadership, communication, motivation and long-term potential.

07

Beyond Placement

Carry forward market intelligence, relationships and lessons into the next search.
