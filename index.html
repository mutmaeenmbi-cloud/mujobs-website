TWO WAYS TO REACH TALENT

More applications—or more relevant conversations?

Job advertising and direct search serve different needs. For a specialist vacancy, the question is whether the right people are seeing—and considering—your opportunity.

THE TRADITIONAL ROUTE

Publish. Wait. Review.

  • Publish a vacancy and reach people who are actively looking.
  • Review the applications that arrive against the role requirements.
  • If the specialist profile is missing, reassess reach and the brief.
THE MUJOBS WAY

Map. Connect. Qualify.

  • Define the experience the business actually needs.
  • Identify relevant people and approach them personally—including passive candidates.
  • Check motivation and role fit before presenting a reasoned shortlist.

THE MUJOBS METHOD

From a business problem to a relevant shortlist.

A transparent process that keeps the role, the evidence and the decision connected.

  1. Define the brief

    Agree responsibilities, must-have evidence and the conditions that make the role realistic.

  2. Map and approach

    Identify relevant employers, build the candidate market and begin personal conversations.

  3. Qualify and coordinate

    Explore experience and motivation, explain the shortlist and coordinate the next steps.

  4. Decide and learn

    Support a clear decision and retain useful market insight for the next search.
