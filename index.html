03 / The mujobs method

A clear process.
A considered search.

From the first conversation to the next chapter, every stage has a purpose.

01

Understand the business

Learn your direction, the challenge and what the hire must solve.

02

Define success

Agree the outcomes, essential capabilities and non-negotiables.

03

Map the market

Identify relevant companies, talent pools and adjacent experience.

04

Search with purpose

Look beyond active applicants and matching job titles.

05

Start meaningful conversations

Approach candidates with preparation, context and personal relevance.

06

Evaluate together

Review fit, motivations and evidence. Coordinate the next steps.

07

Build beyond the hire

Carry forward relationships, pipeline knowledge and search insights.
