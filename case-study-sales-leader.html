Sales Team Build

Building a six-person commercial team of SDR and Account Executive talent for a Berlin SaaS technology startup.

SUCCESSFUL SEARCH
6team hires
SDRpipeline creation
AEclosing talent
SaaSmarket
01THE CHALLENGE

What made the role difficult.

The startup needed to build a sales function, not fill one isolated vacancy. That meant balancing early-pipeline SDR profiles with Account Executives who could run discovery, manage deals and close business in a SaaS environment.

The hiring challenge was consistency: six people needed to fit the same commercial system while bringing different levels of experience and responsibility.

02THE MUJOBS SEARCH

How we solved the search.

mujobs separated the market into SDR and AE talent pools, mapped relevant SaaS companies and qualified candidates around activity, quota evidence, deal ownership, sales methodology and startup fit.

The pipeline was built as a team-building asset rather than six disconnected searches.

03THE OUTCOME

What changed for the client.

The project resulted in a six-person sales team across SDR and Account Executive roles, giving the startup a commercial foundation it could continue to scale.
