Building a SaaS sales team: SDR and Account Executive recruitment

Building a six-person commercial team of SDR and Account Executive talent for a Berlin SaaS technology startup.

01

The hiring challenge

The startup needed to build a sales function, not fill one isolated vacancy. That meant balancing early-pipeline SDR profiles with Account Executives who could run discovery, manage deals and close business in a SaaS environment.

The hiring challenge was consistency: six people needed to fit the same commercial system while bringing different levels of experience and responsibility.

02

The search approach

mujobs separated the market into SDR and AE talent pools, mapped relevant SaaS companies and qualified candidates around activity, quota evidence, deal ownership, sales methodology and startup fit.

The pipeline was built as a team-building asset rather than six disconnected searches.

03

The outcome

The project resulted in a six-person sales team across SDR and Account Executive roles, giving the startup a commercial foundation it could continue to scale.

04

Understanding the role

Building a sales team requires complementary roles. SDRs create and qualify early opportunities; account executives carry the later sales conversation and closing responsibility. The exact boundaries vary by business, so the search should define the handover, customer segment and expectations for each role before combining the individual pipelines into one team plan.

Questions that make the brief more precise

  • Which parts of pipeline generation and closing does each role own?
  • What customer segment and deal complexity are relevant?
  • How will the team work together after the individual hires?
05

Planning a similar search

Start with the work the new person will own, the business context and the constraints that cannot change. Separate essential experience from preferences. That gives the market mapping a clear direction and makes it easier to explain why someone belongs on the shortlist.

A similar title is only a research starting point. In an initial conversation, we would clarify the project or team, the practical conditions and the evidence you need to make a decision. The scope of sourcing, candidate qualification and interview coordination can then be agreed around the actual requirement.
