01THE CHALLENGE

Why the search was difficult.

The client needed a senior profile able to cover end-to-end real-estate commercialization: positioning, sales, customer and investor relationships, contracts, market analysis and coordination with project development.

The role had already been difficult to close for more than six months, so a broad applicant-led approach was not enough.

02THE SEARCH

How we approached the market.

We narrowed the market around real-estate marketing leadership and commercial experience, then used direct search to approach relevant people rather than waiting for active applicants.

The search produced 30 targeted profiles and 22 replies, giving the client a much tighter pool to evaluate.

03THE OUTCOME

What the search produced.

A top candidate was hired within 1.5 weeks. The case became a clear example of what focused direct search can do when a specialist role has been open for months.
